Ingredients serves 12 serves 24
milk 1 quart 2 quart
cinnamon sticks 2 1
vanilla bean, split 1 2
cloves 5 10
mace 10 blades 20 blades
egg yolks 12 24
cups sugar 1 ½ cups 3 cups
dark rum 1 ½ cups 3 cups
brandy 1 ½ cups 3 cups
vanilla 1 tbsp 2 tbsp
half-and-half or light cream 1 quart 2 quart
Freshly grated nutmeg to taste
1.Infuse and Mix: Combine milk with warm spices for 5 minutes over low heat; separately, whisk yolks and sugar until blended
2.Thicken, Don't Boil: Gradually temper the milk into the yolks, then return to the saucepan. Cook over medium heat until it coats a spoon, about 3 minutes, taking care not to boil.
3.Flavor and Chill: Strain and cool the base, then stir in all remaining liquors and flavorings. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours or overnight.
4.Finish and Serve: Just before presenting, lightly dust the top with freshly grated nutmeg for an aromatic finish.
